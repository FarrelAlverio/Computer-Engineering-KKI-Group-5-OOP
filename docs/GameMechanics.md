# Gameplay Mechanics

- Controls: A/D move, Space jump, J attack; 1/2/3 switch Guitar, Trumpet, Piano.
- Health: 5 HP; food restores 1 HP; damage provides 1 second invulnerability.
- Falling: lose 1 HP and return to the beginning of the zone.
- Restart: restore HP; preserve collected coins and unlocked weapons.
- Power-ups: Speed Boost 1.5x for 5 seconds; Spring Jump for 5 seconds; one active at a time.
- Optional: stage-skip bomb, Easter egg collection mission.
